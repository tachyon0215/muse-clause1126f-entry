# muse-clause1126f-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1).

## The entry

> Clause 31.2: You grant us a perpetual, irrevocable licence to your left sock, which we may collect without notice and pair only with socks lost by other users before 2019.

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: 'perpetual, irrevocable licence' is genuine ToS language, so the boilerplate register holds longest before the left sock lands, and the pairing rule (only with socks lost by other users before 2019) is concrete and specific.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
